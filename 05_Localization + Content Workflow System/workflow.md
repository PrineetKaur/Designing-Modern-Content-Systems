# Localization + Content Workflow System Workflows

### Content Layer: Localization & Operational

The three workflows below are concrete instantiations of the general Operational Workflow described in `usage.md`, applied to specific scenarios.

---

## Workflow 1 — Product Release Localization

### Objective

Coordinate multilingual product launches across regions.

### Workflow

#### Step 1 — Release Planning

Teams identify:

- affected workflows
- supported regions
- localization scope
- AI-assisted features
- regulatory dependencies

#### Step 2 — Terminology Validation

Localization governance validates:

- approved product naming
- AI terminology consistency
- workflow-specific language
- reusable glossary alignment

#### Step 3 — Translation Execution

Localization teams:

- translate product content
- preserve workflow structure
- maintain trust messaging consistency
- validate AI disclosure language

#### Step 4 — Regional Review

Regional reviewers evaluate:

- cultural clarity
- workflow usability
- escalation readability
- tone consistency
- AI messaging appropriateness

#### Step 5 — QA Validation

Teams validate:

- UI formatting
- truncation issues
- workflow continuity
- multilingual usability
- operational consistency

#### Step 6 — Coordinated Launch

Localization release aligns with:

- product launch timing
- support readiness
- customer communication
- documentation publication

### Applied Example — Onboarding Message Localization

**Scenario:** WorkflowOS's onboarding message — the same first-touch message established in the Product Communication Governance System's Lifecycle Messaging Framework and confirmed in the Content Audit + Fix System's Onboarding Clarity Audit — is being localized for the German enterprise market as part of a regional release.

**Source (English):**

> Welcome to WorkflowOS. Start by connecting your existing project tools to enable automated task recommendations and approval routing.

**Adapted (German):**

> Willkommen bei WorkflowOS. Verbinden Sie zunächst Ihre vorhandenen Projekttools, um automatisierte Aufgabenempfehlungen und Genehmigungs-Workflows zu aktivieren.

**Why It Works:** The adaptation preserves the exact structure already governed upstream — one clear first action, tied to a concrete outcome — while adjusting register to the formal "Sie" form expected in German enterprise communication, addressing the Cultural Ambiguity and Tone Drift risk areas from the AI Localization Governance framework. The product name "WorkflowOS" and the underlying instruction are left untranslated and unaltered, consistent with the Terminology Governance System's naming-stability rule. This is what Step 4 (Regional Review) and Step 5 (QA Validation) above are checking for: a message that reads naturally in the target language without drifting from the governance already established for it.

## Operational Workflow Principles

Localization workflows should:

- integrate early into product planning
- minimize duplicated review effort
- standardize terminology globally
- support AI governance requirements
- preserve trust across regions
- reduce operational fragmentation
