# Product Communication Governance System Usage Guide

### Content Layer: Product Communications

---
# Purpose

This document explains how teams operationalize the Product Communication Governance System.

The system supports coordination across:

- product
- engineering
- support operations
- customer success
- legal
- content design
- lifecycle marketing

---

# When Teams Should Use This System

Use this system when creating:

- onboarding communication
- release announcements
- operational notifications
- incident updates
- escalation messaging
- billing communication
- security alerts
- AI-assisted lifecycle messaging
- product change communication

---

# Operational Workflow

## Step 1 — Define Communication Type

Teams identify:

- communication category
- operational severity
- affected audience
- required user action
- escalation risk

---

## Step 2 — Apply Governance Standards

Teams validate:

- terminology consistency
- tone alignment
- trust implications
- operational accuracy
- disclosure requirements

---

## Step 3 — Align Cross-Channel Messaging
Content should remain behaviorally aligned across:

- email
- in-product notifications
- support messaging
- release notes
- status pages
- onboarding flows

---

## Step 4 — Review Escalation Risk

Teams evaluate:

- business continuity impact
- customer trust sensitivity
- enterprise communication requirements
- legal or compliance implications

---

## Step 5 — Publish and Monitor

Teams monitor:

- customer confusion signals
- support ticket spikes
- engagement metrics
- escalation rates
- terminology consistency

# Anti-Patterns

## Avoid Vague Operational Messaging

Bad:

> Improvements are coming soon.

---

## Avoid False Certainty
Bad:

> This update will completely eliminate delays.

---

## Avoid Hidden Impact

Bad:

> Temporary issue detected.

Without clarifying:

- affected workflows
- customer impact
- expected resolution

---

## Avoid Alarmist Security Messaging

Bad:

> Your account may have been compromised.

Without:

- context
- evidence
- recovery guidance

---

# Operational Review Checklist

Before publishing communications, teams should verify:

- product behavior matches messaging
- terminology aligns across systems
- urgency level is accurate
- escalation paths are clear
- recovery guidance exists
- AI disclosures are visible where necessary
- customer action requirements are explicit

---
