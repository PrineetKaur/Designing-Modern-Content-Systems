# Content Audit + Fix System Usage Guide

### Content Layer: Shared Principles & Governance

---

## Purpose

This document explains how teams operationalize the Content Audit + Fix System.

The system supports collaboration across:

- content design
- product design
- engineering
- support operations
- localization teams
- AI governance teams
- legal and compliance

---

## When Teams Should Use This System

Use this system when:

- evaluating UX content quality
- reviewing AI-assisted workflows
- auditing onboarding experiences
- standardizing terminology
- reducing support confusion
- preparing localization workflows
- reviewing operational communication
- improving error recovery patterns

---

## Operational Workflow

### Step 1 — Inventory Content Systems

Teams identify:

- product surfaces
- communication categories
- AI workflows
- operational dependencies
- customer-facing content risks

### Step 2 — Run Structured Audit Reviews

Reviewers evaluate:

- UX clarity
- trust communication
- terminology consistency
- accessibility
- localization readiness
- AI disclosure quality

### Step 3 — Assign Severity Levels

Issues are categorized by:

- workflow impact
- operational risk
- customer trust exposure
- scalability implications

### Step 4 — Prioritize Remediation

Teams prioritize fixes based on:

- enterprise impact
- support burden
- workflow criticality
- AI risk exposure
- operational scale

### Step 5 — Validate Governance Alignment

Updated content is reviewed against:

- UX writing standards
- communication governance systems
- terminology frameworks
- localization constraints
- AI governance policies

## Anti-Patterns

### Avoid Subjective-Only Reviews

Bad:

> This wording feels unclear.

Without measurable evaluation criteria.

### Avoid Governance Without Prioritization

Bad:

- fixing low-impact wording
- ignoring enterprise trust risks
- ignoring AI disclosure failures

### Avoid Terminology Drift

Bad:

Using multiple labels for identical workflows across systems.

### Avoid Isolated UX Reviews

Bad:

Reviewing UI copy without considering:

- lifecycle communication
- localization impact
- AI workflows
- operational escalation

---

## Operational Review Checklist

Before approving content updates, teams should verify:

- terminology is standardized
- AI behavior is transparent
- recovery guidance exists
- escalation paths are clear
- readability is accessible
- localization risks are minimized
- operational consistency is preserved
