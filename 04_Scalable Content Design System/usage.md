# Scalable Content Design System Usage Guide

### Content Layer: Shared Principles & Governance

---

## Purpose

This document explains how teams operationalize the Scalable Content Design System.

The system supports:

- product teams
- content design
- engineering
- localization
- AI governance
- support operations
- lifecycle communications

---

## When Teams Should Use This System

Use this system when:

- designing reusable UX patterns
- standardizing terminology
- creating governance frameworks
- scaling AI-assisted workflows
- operationalizing localization readiness
- building reusable onboarding systems
- reviewing trust communication
- standardizing operational messaging

---

## Operational Workflow

This is the workflow teams follow when applying already-approved patterns and templates to a specific task. For how new patterns get created and approved in the first place, see `system.md`'s Review Workflow.

### Step 1 — Identify Workflow Type

Teams determine:

- product surface
- communication category
- AI involvement level
- operational risk
- localization impact

### Step 2 — Select Reusable Framework

Teams apply:

- shared templates
- governance patterns
- tone systems
- terminology standards
- recovery structures

### Step 3 — Validate Governance Alignment

Reviewers evaluate:

- consistency
- trust clarity
- accessibility
- localization readiness
- AI disclosure compliance

### Step 4 — Publish Reusable Patterns

Approved content becomes:

- reusable operational guidance
- scalable design system patterns
- governance references

## Anti-Patterns

### Avoid Governance Without Operational Adoption

Bad:

- static style guides
- unused documentation
- isolated editorial rules

### Avoid Pattern Fragmentation

Bad:

Different onboarding structures across identical workflows.

### Avoid Tone Inconsistency

Bad:

- robotic product UI
- overly promotional lifecycle messaging
- inconsistent escalation communication

### Avoid AI Governance Gaps

Bad:

- hidden automation
- misleading AI certainty
- inconsistent disclosure behavior

---

## Operational Review Checklist

Before approving reusable patterns, teams should verify:

- terminology is standardized
- templates follow governance standards
- AI behaviors are transparent
- localization constraints are considered
- accessibility requirements are met
- escalation guidance exists where needed

---

## Success Indicators

The system is working effectively when:

- governance adoption increases
- duplicated UX patterns decrease
- terminology consistency improves
- localization rework decreases
- support confusion decreases
- AI trust satisfaction improves
