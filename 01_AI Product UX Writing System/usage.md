# AI Product UX Writing System Usage Guide

### Content Layer: In-Product Experience

---

# Purpose

This document explains how product teams operationalize the AI Product UX Writing System.

The system is designed for cross-functional collaboration across:

- product design
- engineering
- content design
- AI/ML teams
- support operations
- localization teams

---

# When Teams Should Use This System

Use this system when designing:

- AI copilots
- onboarding flows
- AI-generated recommendations
- workflow automation
- AI summaries
- predictive features
- error states
- empty states
- conversational interfaces
- escalation workflows

---

# Workflow Integration

## Step 1 — Define User Intent

Teams identify:

- user goal
- workflow context
- AI involvement
- potential trust risks

---
## Step 2 — Select Interaction Pattern

Teams determine:

| Pattern Type | Use Case |
|---|---|
| Assistive | accelerating tasks |
| Suggestive | recommendations |
| Predictive | forecasting |
| Automated | system-generated actions |
| Conversational | chat-based interaction |

---

## Step 3 — Apply Trust Framework

Teams ensure:

- transparency
- explainability
- recovery guidance
- user review opportunities

---

## Step 4 — Validate Terminology

Content should align with:

- shared product vocabulary
- governance standards
- localization constraints

---

## Step 5 — Review Edge Cases

Teams review:

- uncertainty handling
- unsupported inputs
- escalation scenarios
- AI failure states

---

# Collaboration Model

| Team | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Design | interaction patterns |
| Content Design | UX writing governance |
| Engineering | implementation logic |
| AI/ML Teams | model behavior alignment |
| Legal | disclosure requirements |
| Support | escalation feedback |

---
# Operational Review Checklist

Before launch, teams should verify:

- AI behavior is explained
- terminology is consistent
- error recovery exists
- automation boundaries are clear
- tone matches governance standards
- user agency is preserved

---

# Anti-Patterns

## Avoid Vague AI Messaging

Bad:

> Smart recommendations available.

---

## Avoid False Certainty

Bad:

> This is the best solution.

---

## Avoid Dead-End Errors

Bad:

> Request failed.

---

## Avoid Hidden Automation

Bad:

> Changes applied.

Without explaining:

- what changed
- why changes occurred
- whether review is required

---

# Success Indicators
The system is working effectively when:

- support tickets decrease
- AI adoption improves
- onboarding completion increases
- user trust scores improve
- terminology consistency improves
- design review cycles become faster
