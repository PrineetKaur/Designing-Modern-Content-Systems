# AI Product UX Writing System Examples

### Content Layer: In-Product Experience

---

# Example 1 — AI Copilot Prompt

## Before

> Ask anything.

---

## Problems

- vague
- unclear expectations
- no workflow guidance
- no AI limitation framing

---

## After

> Describe the workflow you want to automate. The assistant may suggest steps based on recent activity, but you’ll review changes before publishing.

---

# Example 2 — AI Recommendation

## Before

> Recommended action available.

---

## Problems

- unclear recommendation source
- no trust framing
- lacks transparency

---

## After

> This recommendation is based on recent project activity and similar completed workflows.

---

# Example 3 — Error Recovery

## Before

> Something went wrong.

---

## Problems
- no diagnosis
- no recovery guidance
- increases frustration

---

## After

> We couldn’t generate a summary because the selected report contains unsupported file types. Upload a PDF, DOCX, or TXT file and try again.

---

# Example 4 — Empty State

## Before

> No automations found.

---

## Problems

- dead-end interaction
- lacks workflow guidance

---

## After

> You haven’t created any automations yet. Start with a workflow template or describe a repetitive task for the assistant to help automate.

---

# Example 5 — AI Disclosure

## Before

> Campaign generated successfully.

---

## Problems

- unclear AI involvement
- no review expectation

---

## After

> Draft campaign generated using your recent customer engagement data. Review messaging before publishing.

---

# Example 6 — Uncertainty Handling

## Before

> Best option identified.

---

## Problems
- misleading certainty
- overpromises AI capability

---

## After

> This option may improve workflow completion time based on recent usage patterns.

---

# Example 7 — Escalation Guidance

## Before

> Failed to process request.

---

## After

> We couldn’t complete the request because your workspace permissions changed during processing. Refresh the page and try again. If the issue continues, contact your workspace administrator.

---

# Cross-System Design Insight

These examples demonstrate how mature UX writing systems support:

- trust
- transparency
- governance
- scalability
- operational consistency

The goal is not simply “better microcopy.”

The goal is scalable interaction reliability.
